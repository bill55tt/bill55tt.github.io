Analysis of Transient Current Formula in RL Discharge Circuit

Overview

This comprehensive article provides a detailed analysis of the transient current formula in RL discharge circuits. We examine the fundamental principles of inductor discharge, the mathematical relationships governing current decay, and the physical interpretation of the time constant in RL circuits.

RL Discharge Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

Basic RL Discharge Circuit:

  • Inductor: Stores magnetic energy during charging phase
  • Resistor: Provides discharge path for stored energy
  • Switch: Controls transition from charging to discharging state
  • Power Supply: Provides initial energy for charging (disconnected during discharge)

Switch Operation

Position 1 (Charging State):

  • Circuit State: Power supply connected to inductor
  • Inductor Behavior: Accumulates magnetic energy
  • Current: Builds up to steady-state value
  • Energy Storage: Maximum magnetic energy stored

Position 2 (Discharging State):

  • Circuit State: Power supply disconnected
  • Inductor Behavior: Releases stored magnetic energy
  • Current: Decays exponentially from initial value
  • Energy Transfer: From inductor to resistor

Transient Current Analysis

1. Initial Conditions

When Switch Moves from Position 1 to Position 2:

  • Power Supply: Removed from circuit
  • Inductor Current: Cannot change instantaneously (Lenz’s law)
  • Initial Current: $ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} $
  • Energy Conservation: Magnetic energy maintains current flow

2. Current Decay Characteristics

Exponential Decay Pattern:

  • Initial Value: $ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} $ (100% of steady-state current)
  • Decay Formula: $ i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • Final Value: $ i(\infty) = 0 $ (complete discharge)

3. Time Constant Analysis

Mathematical Definition:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$
Physical Interpretation:

  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Meaning: Time for current to decay to 36.8% of initial value
  • Circuit Response: Characterizes discharge rate
  • Energy Dissipation: Controls power transfer rate

Mathematical Derivation

1. Circuit Equation

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL):
$$ V_R(t) + V_L(t) = 0 $$

Component Voltages:

  • Resistor Voltage: $ V_R(t) = i(t) \times R $
  • Inductor Voltage: $ V_L(t) = L \frac{di(t)}{dt} $

Differential Equation:
$$ i(t)R + L \frac{di(t)}{dt} = 0 $$

2. Solution Method

Rearranging the Equation:
$$ L \frac{di(t)}{dt} = -i(t)R $$

$$ \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} dt $$

Integration:
$$ \int \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} \int dt $$

$$ \ln i(t) = -\frac{R}{L} t + C $$

Exponential Form:
$$ i(t) = e^{-\frac{R}{L} t + C} = e^C \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Initial Condition Application:
$$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} = e^C $$

Final Formula:
$$ i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Time-Dependent Behavior

Key Time Points

t = 0 (Discharge Start):

  • Current: $ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} $ (100% of initial current)

  • Energy: Maximum magnetic energy stored

  • Power: Maximum power dissipation in resistor

  • Current: $ i(\tau) = 0.368 \frac{E}{R} $ (36.8% of initial current)

  • Energy: 63.2% of initial energy dissipated

  • Power: Reduced to 13.5% of initial power

  • Current: $ i(2\tau) = 0.135 \frac{E}{R} $ (13.5% of initial current)

  • Energy: 86.5% of initial energy dissipated

  • Power: Reduced to 1.8% of initial power

  • Current: $ i(3\tau) = 0.050 \frac{E}{R} $ (5.0% of initial current)

  • Energy: 95.0% of initial energy dissipated

  • Power: Reduced to 0.25% of initial power

  • Current: $ i(5\tau) \approx 0 $ (effectively zero)

  • Energy: 99.3% of initial energy dissipated

  • Power: Negligible power dissipation

Energy Considerations

1. Initial Magnetic Energy

Stored Energy:
$$ W_L = \frac{1}{2} L i^2(0) = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

2. Power Dissipation

Instantaneous Power:
$$ P_R(t) = i^2(t) R = \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 R e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} = \frac{E^2}{R} e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} $$

3. Total Energy Dissipated

Energy Conservation:
$$ W_{total} = \int_0^{\infty} P_R(t) dt = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

Verification:
$$ W_{total} = \frac{E^2}{R} \int_0^{\infty} e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} dt = \frac{E^2}{R} \cdot \frac{\tau}{2} = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

Circuit Analysis Examples

Example 1: Simple RL Circuit

Given:

  • Inductor: L = 50 mH
  • Resistor: R = 25 Ω
  • Supply Voltage: E = 10 V

Calculate:

  1. Time constant
  2. Initial current
  3. Current at t = 2τ
  4. Time for current to reach 10% of initial value

Solution:
1. Time Constant:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{50 \times 10^{-3}}{25} = 2ms $$

2. Initial Current:
$$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} = \frac{10}{25} = 0.4A $$

3. Current at t = 2τ:
$$ i(2\tau) = 0.4 \cdot e^{-2} = 0.4 \cdot 0.135 = 0.054A $$

4. Time for 10% Current:
$$ 0.1 = e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$
$$ \ln(0.1) = -\frac{t}{\tau} $$
$$ t = -\tau \ln(0.1) = 2 \times 2.303 = 4.61ms $$

Example 2: Complex RL Circuit

Given:

  • Inductor: L = 100 mH
  • R₁ = 20 Ω, R₂ = 30 Ω
  • Supply Voltage: E = 15 V

Calculate:

  1. Effective resistance during discharge
  2. Time constant
  3. Initial current
  4. Current after 5\text{ ms}

Solution:
1. Effective Resistance:
$$ R_{eff} = R_1 + R_2 = 20 + 30 = 50 \Omega $$

2. Time Constant:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R_{eff}} = \frac{100 \times 10^{-3}}{50} = 2ms $$

3. Initial Current:
$$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R_{eff}} = \frac{15}{50} = 0.3A $$

4. Current after 5\text{ ms}:
$$ t = 5ms = 2.5\tau $$
$$ i(5ms) = 0.3 \cdot e^{-2.5} = 0.3 \cdot 0.082 = 0.025A $$

RL 放電電路基礎

電路配置

基本 RL 放電電路：

  • 電感器：充電階段儲存磁能
  • 電阻：為儲存能量提供放電路徑
  • 開關：控制從充電到放電狀態的轉換
  • 電源：為充電提供初始能量（放電期間斷開）

開關操作

位置 1（充電狀態）：

  • 電路狀態：電源連接到電感器
  • 電感行為：累積磁能
  • 電流：建立到穩態值
  • 能量儲存：最大磁能儲存

位置 2（放電狀態）：

  • 電路狀態：電源斷開
  • 電感行為：釋放儲存的磁能
  • 電流：從初始值指數衰減
  • 能量轉移：從電感器到電阻

暫態電流分析

1. 初始條件

當開關從位置 1 移至位置 2 時：

  • 電源：從電路中移除
  • 電感電流：不能瞬間變化（楞次定理）
  • 初始電流：$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} $
  • 能量守恆：磁能維持電流流動

2. 電流衰減特性

指數衰減模式：

  • 初始值：$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} $（穩態電流的 100%）
  • 衰減公式：$ i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • 最終值：$ i(\infty) = 0 $（完全放電）

3. 時間常數分析

數學定義：
$$
\tau = \frac{L}{R}
$$

物理解釋：

  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 意義：電流衰減到初始值 36.8% 的時間
  • 電路響應：表徵放電速率
  • 能量消耗：控制功率轉移速率

數學推導

1. 電路方程式

基爾霍夫電壓定律（KVL）：
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

元件電壓：

  • 電阻電壓：$ V_R(t) = i(t) \times R $
  • 電感電壓：$ V_L(t) = L \frac{di(t)}{dt} $

微分方程式：
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R}$$

2. 求解方法

重新排列方程式：
$$ L \frac{di(t)}{dt} = -i(t)R $$

$$ \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} dt $$

積分：
$$ \int \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} \int dt $$

$$ \ln i(t) = -\frac{R}{L} t + C $$

指數形式：
$$ i(t) = e^{-\frac{R}{L} t + C} = e^C \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

初始條件應用：
$$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} = e^C $$

最終公式：
$$ i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

能量考量

1. 初始磁能

儲存能量：
$$ W_L = \frac{1}{2} L i^2(0) = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

3. 總能量消耗

驗證：
$$ W_{total} = \frac{E^2}{R} \int_0^{\infty} e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} dt = \frac{E^2}{R} \cdot \frac{\tau}{2} = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

Analysis of Resistor and Inductor Voltage Formulas in RL Discharge Transient

Overview

This comprehensive article provides detailed analysis of voltage formulas for both resistor and inductor components in RL discharge transient circuits. We examine the mathematical relationships, physical interpretations, and practical applications of exponential decay in RL discharge phenomena.

RL Discharge Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

Basic RL Discharge Circuit:

  • Inductor: Stores magnetic energy
  • Resistor: Dissipates energy during discharge
  • Switch: Controls discharge initiation
  • Initial Conditions: Inductor carries initial current

Discharge Process Overview

When the switch is opened in an RL circuit:

  1. Energy Transfer: Magnetic energy stored in inductor transfers to resistor
  2. Current Decay: Current decreases exponentially over time
  3. Voltage Changes: Both inductor and resistor voltages follow exponential decay
  4. Steady State: Circuit reaches zero current and voltage

Mathematical Foundation

Time Constant Definition

The time constant for RL circuits is defined as:

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

Physical Interpretation:

  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Meaning: Time for current to decay to 36.8% of initial value
  • Circuit Behavior: Characterizes discharge rate

Current Decay Formula

The current in an RL discharge circuit follows:

$$ i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Where:

  • $ i(t) $：Current at time t
  • $ I_0 $：Initial current
  • $ t $：Time after switch opening
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $

Resistor Voltage Analysis

Resistor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the resistor during RL discharge is:

$$ V_R(t) = E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Formula Components:

  • $ V_R(t) $：Resistor voltage at time t
  • $ E $：Initial voltage (same as initial inductor voltage)
  • $ t $：Time after discharge begins
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $

Physical Interpretation

Resistor Voltage Characteristics:

  • Decay Pattern: Exponential decrease over time
  • Final Value: $ V_R(\infty) = 0 $ (zero voltage at steady state)
  • Energy Dissipation: Power dissipated as heat in resistor

  • t = 3\tau: $ V_R(3\tau) = 0.050E $ (5.0% of initial voltage)
  • t = 5\tau: $ V_R(5\tau) \approx 0 $ (effectively zero)

Inductor Voltage Analysis

Inductor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the inductor during RL discharge is:

$$ V_L(t) = -E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Formula Components:

  • $ V_L(t) $：Inductor voltage at time t
  • $ E $：Initial voltage magnitude
  • $ t $：Time after discharge begins
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $
  • Negative Sign: Indicates opposite polarity to resistor voltage

Physical Interpretation

Inductor Voltage Characteristics:

  • Initial Value: $ V_L(0) = -E $ (negative of initial voltage)
  • Decay Pattern: Exponential decrease in magnitude
  • Final Value: $ V_L(\infty) = 0 $ (zero voltage at steady state)
  • Polarity: Opposite to resistor voltage to maintain current flow

Polarity Explanation

Why Negative Sign:

  • Lenz’s Law: Inductor opposes change in current
  • Current Direction: During discharge, current decreases
  • Voltage Polarity: Inductor generates voltage to oppose current decrease
  • Energy Conservation: Maintains circuit equilibrium

Voltage Relationship Analysis

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law Verification

At any time t, the sum of voltages around the loop must equal zero:

$$ V_R(t) + V_L(t) = 0 $$

Substituting Formulas:
$$ E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + (-E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) = 0 $$

This confirms that our formulas satisfy KVL.

Voltage Magnitude Relationship

Equal Magnitudes:

  • Resistor Voltage: $ |V_R(t)| = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • Inductor Voltage: $ |V_L(t)| = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • Relationship: $ |V_R(t)| = |V_L(t)| $ at all times

Opposite Polarity:

  • Resistor: Positive voltage drop
  • Inductor: Negative voltage drop
  • Net Effect: Zero total voltage around loop

This confirms that all stored energy is dissipated in the resistor.

RL 放電電路基礎

電路配置

基本 RL 放電電路：

  • 電阻：放電期間消耗能量
  • 開關：控制放電開始
  • 初始條件：電感器攜帶初始電流

放電過程概述

當 RL 電路中的開關打開時：

  1. 能量轉移：儲存在電感器中的磁能轉移到電阻
  2. 電流衰減：電流隨時間指數衰減
  3. 電壓變化：電感和電阻電壓都遵循指數衰減
  4. 穩態：電路達到零電流和零電壓

數學基礎

時間常數定義

RL 電路的時間常數定義為：

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

物理解釋：

  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 意義：電流衰減到初始值 36.8% 的時間
  • 電路行為：表徵放電速率

電流衰減公式

RL 放電電路中的電流遵循：

$$ i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

其中：

  • $ i(t) $：時間 t 時的電流
  • $ I_0 $：初始電流
  • $ t $：開關打開後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $

電阻電壓分析

電阻電壓公式

RL 放電期間電阻兩端的電壓為：

$$ V_R(t) = E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

公式組成：

  • $ V_R(t) $：時間 t 時的電阻電壓
  • $ E $：初始電壓（與初始電感電壓相同）
  • $ t $：放電開始後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $

物理解釋

電阻電壓特性：

  • 初始值：$ V_R(0) = E $（最大電壓）
  • 衰減模式：隨時間指數衰減
  • 最終值：$ V_R(\infty) = 0 $（穩態時零電壓）
  • 能量消耗：功率在電阻中以熱能形式消耗

時間相關行為

關鍵時間點：

  • t = 0：$ V_R(0) = E $（初始電壓的 100%）
  • t = \tau：$ V_R(\tau) = 0.368E $（初始電壓的 36.8%）
  • t = 2\tau：$ V_R(2\tau) = 0.135E $（初始電壓的 13.5%）
  • t = 3\tau：$ V_R(3\tau) = 0.050E $（初始電壓的 5.0%）
  • t = 5\tau：$ V_R(5\tau) \approx 0 $（實質上為零）

電感電壓分析

電感電壓公式

RL 放電期間電感兩端的電壓為：

$$ V_L(t) = -E e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

公式組成：

  • $ V_L(t) $：時間 t 時的電感電壓
  • $ E $：初始電壓大小
  • $ t $：放電開始後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $
  • 負號：表示與電阻電壓相反的極性

物理解釋

電感電壓特性：

  • 初始值：$ V_L(0) = -E $（初始電壓的負值）
  • 衰減模式：大小指數衰減
  • 最終值：$ V_L(\infty) = 0 $（穩態時零電壓）
  • 極性：與電阻電壓相反以維持電流流動

極性解釋

為什麼是負號：

  • 楞次定律：電感器反對電流變化
  • 電流方向：放電期間，電流減少
  • 電壓極性：電感器產生電壓以反對電流減少
  • 能量守恆：維持電路平衡

電壓關係分析

基爾霍夫電壓定律驗證

在任何時間 t，迴路中電壓總和必須等於零：

這確認了我們的公式滿足 KVL。

  • 電感電壓：$ |V_L(t)| = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • 關係：$ |V_R(t)| = |V_L(t)| $ 在所有時間

相反極性：

  • 電阻：正電壓降
  • 電感：負電壓降
  • 淨效應：迴路中總電壓為零

RL Charging Transient Basic Formulas and Example Analysis

Overview

This comprehensive article provides detailed analysis of the fundamental formulas governing RL charging transient behavior and their application in practical example problems. We examine the mathematical relationships between current and voltage components, time constant calculations, and step-by-step problem-solving strategies.

RL Charging Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

In a typical RL charging circuit:

  • Voltage Source: $ E $ (DC power supply)
  • Resistor: $ R $ (current limiting element)
  • Inductor: $ L $ (energy storage element)
  • Switch: Controls circuit operation
  • Time Constant: $ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $ (characteristic charging time)

Charging Process Overview

The charging process involves:

  1. Initial State: Inductor acts as open circuit, no current flow
  2. Current Build-up: Current increases exponentially over time
  3. Voltage Distribution: Voltage gradually transfers from inductor to resistor
  4. Steady State: Current reaches maximum value $ \frac{E}{R} $

Fundamental Formulas

1. RL Charging Transient Current Formula

The current in an RL charging circuit follows the exponential growth function:

$$ i(t) = I_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

Where:

  • $ i(t) $：Current at time t
  • $ I_{\infty} $：Steady-state current $ = \frac{E}{R} $
  • $ t $：Time after switch closure
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $

Physical Interpretation:

  • Current rises from 0 to steady-state value over time
  • Exponential growth pattern
  • Rate controlled by time constant

2. Resistor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the resistor follows the same exponential growth pattern:

$$ V_R(t) = V_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

Where:

  • $ V_R(t) $：Resistor voltage at time t
  • $ V_{\infty} $：Steady-state voltage $ = E $
  • $ t $：Time after switch closure
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $

Physical Interpretation:

  • Resistor voltage rises from 0 to steady-state value
  • Follows same exponential pattern as current
  • Directly proportional to current (Ohm’s law)

3. Inductor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the inductor follows exponential decay:

$$ V_L(t) = V_0 \times e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$
Where:

  • $ V_L(t) $：Inductor voltage at time t
  • $ V_0 $：Initial voltage $ = E $
  • $ t $：Time after switch closure
  • $ \tau $：Time constant $ = \frac{L}{R} $

Physical Interpretation:

  • Inductor voltage decays from initial value to 0
  • Exponential decay pattern
  • Opposite behavior to resistor voltage

Time Constant Calculation

Definition

The time constant for RL circuits is:

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

Physical Significance

  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Meaning: Time for current to reach 63.2% of final value
  • Growth Rate: Determines how quickly current builds up
  • Circuit Response: Characterizes the circuit’s transient behavior

Example Calculation

Given: $ L = 60mH $, $ R = 20,\Omega $

Time Constant:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{60mH}{20Ω} = \frac{0.06H}{20Ω} = 0.003s = 3ms $$

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law Verification

Circuit Equation

At any time t:
$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E $$

Formula Verification

Substituting the formulas:
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) = E $$

Simplifying:
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E - E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E $$

This confirms that the formulas satisfy Kirchhoff’s voltage law.

基本公式

1. RL充電暫態電流公式

RL 充電電路中的電流遵循指數增長函數：

$$ i(t) = I_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$
其中：

  • $ i(t) $：時間 t 時的電流
  • $ I_{\infty} $：穩態電流 $ = \frac{E}{R} $
  • $ t $：開關閉合後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $

物理解釋：

  • 電流隨時間從 0 增加到穩態值
  • 指數增長模式
  • 速率由時間常數控制

2. 電阻電壓公式

電阻兩端電壓遵循相同的指數增長模式：

$$ V_R(t) = V_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

其中：

  • $ V_R(t) $：時間 t 時的電阻電壓
  • $ V_{\infty} $：穩態電壓 $ = E $
  • $ t $：開關閉合後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $

物理解釋：

  • 電阻電壓從 0 增加到穩態值
  • 與電流遵循相同的指數模式
  • 與電流成正比（歐姆定律）

3. 電感電壓公式

電感兩端電壓遵循指數衰減：

$$ V_L(t) = V_0 \times e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

其中：

  • $ V_L(t) $：時間 t 時的電感電壓
  • $ V_0 $：初始電壓 $ = E $
  • $ t $：開關閉合後的時間
  • $ \tau $：時間常數 $ = \frac{L}{R} $

物理解釋：

  • 電感電壓從初始值衰減到 0
  • 指數衰減模式
  • 與電阻電壓相反的行為

定義

RL 電路的時間常數為：

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

電路方程式

Analysis of Inductor Voltage Formula in RL Charging Circuit Transient

Overview

This comprehensive article provides detailed analysis of the inductor voltage behavior in RL charging circuits during transient conditions. We examine the mathematical relationships between inductor and resistor voltages, the exponential decay characteristics, and the practical implications of inductor voltage changes during the charging process.

RL Charging Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

In a typical RL charging circuit:

  • Voltage Source: $ E $ (DC power supply)
  • Resistor: $ R $ (current limiting element)
  • Inductor: $ L $ (energy storage element)
  • Switch: Controls circuit operation
  • Time Constant: $ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $ (characteristic charging time)

Charging Process Overview

The charging process involves:

  1. Initial State: Inductor acts as open circuit, no current flow
  2. Current Build-up: Current increases exponentially over time
  3. Voltage Distribution: Voltage gradually transfers from inductor to resistor
  4. Steady State: Current reaches maximum value $ \frac{E}{R} $

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law Foundation

Circuit Voltage Relationship

According to Kirchhoff’s voltage law, at any time t:
$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E $$

Voltage Distribution Principle

  • Total Voltage: Always equals source voltage E
  • Voltage Transfer: Gradually shifts from inductor to resistor
  • Conservation: Sum of component voltages remains constant

Inductor Voltage Formula Derivation

Method 1: Direct from Kirchhoff’s Law

Using Kirchhoff’s voltage law and the resistor voltage formula:

$$ V_L(t) = E - V_R(t) $$

Substituting the resistor voltage formula:
$$ V_L(t) = E - E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

Simplifying:
$$ V_L(t) = E - E + E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Method 2: From Inductor Current Relationship

Using the inductor voltage-current relationship:
$$ V_L(t) = L \frac{di}{dt} $$

Substituting the current rate of change:
$$ V_L(t) = L \cdot \frac{E}{L} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Final Inductor Voltage Formula

The inductor voltage follows the exponential decay function:

$$ V_L(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Formula Parameter Analysis

Parameter Definitions

$ E $：Initial inductor voltage at switch closure

  • Units: Volts (V)
  • Dependence: Directly proportional to source voltage
  • Initial Value: $ V_L(0) = E $

$ t $：Time elapsed after switch closure

  • Physical Meaning: Duration of charging process
  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Range: $ 0 \leq t < \infty $

$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $：Time constant

  • Physical Meaning: Characteristic time for voltage to decay to 36.8% of initial value
  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Dependence: Proportional to inductance, inversely proportional to resistance

  • Large L: Longer time constant, slower voltage decay
  • Large R: Shorter time constant, faster voltage decay
  • Small L: Shorter time constant, faster voltage decay
  • Small R: Longer time constant, slower voltage decay

Time-Dependent Voltage Behavior

Voltage Decay Characteristics

The inductor voltage exhibits specific behavior patterns:

  1. Initial State: Maximum voltage across inductor
  2. Exponential Decay: Non-linear decrease following exponential function
  3. Asymptotic Approach: Voltage approaches zero as t → ∞
  4. Time Constant Dependence: L/R determines decay rate

$$ \frac{dV_L}{dt} = -\frac{E}{\tau} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = -\frac{E}{L/R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = -\frac{ER}{L} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

$$
\tau = \frac{L}{R}
$$

電壓分佈原理

  • 總電壓：始終等於電源電壓 E
  • 電壓轉移：逐漸從電感器轉移到電阻
  • 守恆：元件電壓總和保持恆定

電感電壓公式推導

方法一：直接從基爾霍夫定律

使用基爾霍夫電壓定律和電阻電壓公式：

$$ V_L(t) = E - V_R(t) $$

代入電阻電壓公式：
$$ V_L(t) = E - E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

簡化：
$$ V_L(t) = E - E + E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

方法二：從電感電流關係

使用電感電壓電流關係：
$$ V_L(t) = L \frac{di}{dt} $$

代入電流變化率：
$$ V_L(t) = L \cdot \frac{E}{L} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

最終電感電壓公式

電感電壓遵循指數衰減函數：

$$ V_L(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

公式參數分析

參數定義

$ E $：開關閉合瞬間電感兩端的初值電壓

  • 物理意義：電感器上出現的最大電壓
  • 單位：伏特（V）
  • 依賴性：與電源電壓成正比
  • 初始值：$ V_L(0) = E $

$ t $：開關閉合後經過的時間

  • 物理意義：充電過程的持續時間
  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 範圍：$ 0 \leq t < \infty $

$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $：時間常數

  • 物理意義：電壓衰減到初始值 36.8% 的特徵時間
  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 依賴性：與電感成正比，與電阻成反比

參數關係

  • 大 L：較長時間常數，電壓衰減較慢
  • 大 R：較短時間常數，電壓衰減較快
  • 小 L：較短時間常數，電壓衰減較快
  • 小 R：較長時間常數，電壓衰減較慢

時間相關電壓行為

電壓衰減特性

電感電壓表現出特定的行為模式：

  1. 初始狀態：電感器兩端最大電壓
  2. 指數衰減：非線性減少，遵循指數函數
  3. 漸近接近：當 t → ∞ 時電壓接近零
  4. 時間常數依賴：L/R 決定衰減率

數學分析

變化率：
$$ \frac{dV_L}{dt} = -\frac{E}{\tau} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = -\frac{E}{L/R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = -\frac{ER}{L} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

初始速率：
$$ \frac{dV_L}{dt}\bigg|_{t=0} = -\frac{ER}{L} $$

關鍵時間點分析

時間常數倍數

充電期間的重要參考點：

1. 初始狀態（t = 0）

  • 電壓：$ V_L(0) = E $ V

  • 百分比：初始電壓的 100%

  • 行為：電感器兩端最大電壓

  • 電壓：$ V_L(\tau) = E \cdot e^{-1} = 0.368E $

  • 意義：標準參考點

  • 電壓：$ V_L(2\tau) = E \cdot e^{-2} = 0.135E $

  • 電壓：$ V_L(3\tau) = E \cdot e^{-3} = 0.050E $

  • 百分比：初始電壓的 0.7%

  • 意義：暫態基本完成

電路中的電壓關係

基爾霍夫電壓定律驗證

在任何時間 t：
$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) = E $$

元件電壓關係

電感電壓：
$$ V_L(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

電阻電壓：
$$ V_R(t) = E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

電壓分佈

  • 初始狀態：所有電壓在電感器上
  • 最終狀態：所有電壓在電阻上
  • 轉換：從電感器到電阻的逐漸轉移

指數衰減特性

非線性行為

電感電壓遵循指數衰減模式：

  1. 陡峭初始下降：開始時電壓快速減少
  2. 指數衰減：非線性減少，遵循指數函數
  3. 漸近接近：當 t → ∞ 時電壓接近零
  4. 時間常數依賴：L/R 決定衰減率

數學性質

衰減率：

  • 在 t = 0 時最大
  • 隨時間指數衰減
  • 當 t → ∞ 時接近零

曲線形狀：

  • 初始時向下凹
  • 在 t = \tau 處有拐點
  • 隨 t 增加變得更線性

物理解釋

電感器行為

  • 初始反對：電感器反對電流變化（楞次定律）
  • 電壓產生：感應電壓以維持電流連續性
  • 能量儲存：在磁場中儲存能量
  • 逐漸釋放：隨電流建立而釋放能量

電壓產生機制

  • 自感應：電感器產生電壓以反對電流變化
  • 磁場：電壓與電流變化率成正比
  • 能量守恆：能量從電能形式轉換為磁能形式

實際應用

1. 電機啟動

  • 反電動勢：電感電壓反對施加電壓
  • 電流限制：控制初始電流湧浪
  • 保護：防止過大的啟動電流

2. 電力電子

  • DC-DC轉換器：電感電壓控制
  • 開關穩壓器：電壓調節
  • 濾波器設計：電壓平滑應用

3. 電磁裝置

  • 螺線管：受控啟動
  • 繼電器：漸進電流建立
  • 變壓器：初級電壓控制

常見誤解

1. 線性電壓衰減

  • 誤解：電感電壓隨時間線性減少
  • 事實：電壓遵循指數衰減模式

2. 瞬間電壓消失

  • 誤解：電感電壓立即消失
  • 事實：由於電感器特性，電壓逐漸衰減

3. 恆定衰減率

  • 誤解：電壓衰減率保持恆定
  • 事實：衰減率隨時間指數衰減

4. 能量損失

  • 誤解：所有能量在電感器中損失
  • 事實：能量轉移到磁場，然後轉移到電阻

解題策略

1. 識別參數

  • 從問題中提取 E、R、L 值
  • 計算時間常數 $ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $
  • 確定感興趣的時間點

2. 應用電壓公式

  • 使用 $ V_L(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $
  • 代入已知值
  • 計算指定時間的電壓

3. 驗證結果

  • 檢查單位（應為伏特）
  • 確保電壓從 E 減少到 0
  • 驗證物理合理性

4. 交叉檢查計算

  • 使用基爾霍夫電壓定律
  • 驗證電壓關係
  • 檢查時間常數計算
