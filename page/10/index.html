Power Characteristics of Pure Inductive AC Circuit

This article summarizes the power characteristics of pure inductive AC circuits, including phase difference, average power, reactive power, and waveform properties.

Key Points

  1. Voltage and current phase difference is 90 degrees
    Current lags voltage by 90 degrees.

  2. Average power P is zero
    Because $ \cos 90^\circ = 0 $, there is no average power in a pure inductive circuit.

  3. Reactive power $ Q_L $ formulas

$$ Q_L = VI = I^2 X_L = \frac{V^2}{X_L} $$

  1. Maximum instantaneous power $ P_{max} $

$$ P_{max} = VI $$

  1. Minimum instantaneous power $ P_{min} $

$$ P_{min} = -VI $$

  1. Power frequency is twice the source frequency

Pure Inductive AC Circuit and Reactive Power (Part A)

Pure Inductive AC Circuit and Reactive Power (Part A)

This article explains the characteristics of pure inductive AC circuits, the concept of reactive power, and the meaning of the power waveform.

Key Points

  • Pure Inductive AC Circuit: Current lags voltage by 90 degrees.
  • Instantaneous Power $ p(t) $: Calculated as $ p(t) = VI \sin(2\omega t + 180^\circ) $, a sinusoidal wave symmetric about zero.
  • Power Waveform Meaning:
    • Positive waveform: Inductor stores energy.
    • Negative waveform: Inductor releases energy back to the source.
  • Average Power: No average power consumption in pure inductive circuit; power does not contribute to load.
  • Reactive Power (Q): Represents the inductor’s non-working power, unit is var.
  • Reactive Power Calculation:
    • $ Q_L = VI $ (RMS voltage times RMS current)
    • Or $ Q_L = I^2 X_L $
    • Or $ Q_L = \frac{V^2}{X_L} $

純電感交流電路與虛功率（Part A）

本文說明純電感交流電路的特性、虛功率的概念，以及功率波形的意義。

重點歸納

  • 純電感交流電路：電流滯後電壓90度。
  • 瞬間功率 p(t)：計算後為 $ p(t) = VI \sin(2\omega t + 180^\circ) $，為正負對稱的正弦波。
  • 功率波形意義：
    • 正波形：電感儲存能量。
    • 負波形：電感釋放能量回電源。
  • 平均功率：純電感電路無平均功率消耗，功率對負載無貢獻。
  • 虛功率（Q）：代表電感的無效功率，單位為乏爾（var）。
  • 虛功率計算：
    • $ Q_L = VI $（有效值電壓乘以有效值電流）
    • 或 $ Q_L = I^2 X_L $
    • 或 $ Q_L = \frac{V^2}{X_L} $

Power Calculation of Pure Inductive Circuit (Part C Example)

Power Calculation of Pure Inductive Circuit (Part C Example)

This article provides a detailed example of power calculation in a pure inductive circuit, including waveform, inductance, phasor conversion, and all key power formulas.

Key Points

  • Voltage waveform: $ v(t) = 100\sqrt{2} \sin(500t + 30^\circ) $ volts
  • Inductance: 20 mH
  • Calculations:
    1. Phase angle $\theta$
    2. Average power $P$
    3. Inductive reactive power $Q_L$
    4. Maximum instantaneous power $P_{max}$
    5. Minimum instantaneous power $P_{min}$
    6. Instantaneous power frequency $f_p$
  • Phasor conversion:
    • Inductive reactance $X_L = \omega L = 500 \times 0.02 = 10 \Omega$, phase angle 90°
    • Voltage phasor $V = 100 \angle 30^\circ$
    • Current phasor $I = \frac{V}{X_L} = 10 \angle -60^\circ$
  • Phase difference $\theta = 30^\circ - (-60^\circ) = 90^\circ$
  • Average power $P = VI \cos \theta = 100 \times 10 \times \cos 90^\circ = 0$ watts
  • Reactive power $Q_L = I^2 X_L = 10^2 \times 10 = 1000$ vars
  • Maximum instantaneous power $P_{max} = VI = 1000$ watts
  • Minimum instantaneous power $P_{min} = -VI = -1000$ watts
  • Supply frequency $f = \frac{500}{2\pi} \approx 80$ Hz
  • Power frequency $f_p = 2 \times 80 = 160$ Hz

Pure Resistive AC Circuit and Average Power

This article explains the characteristics of pure resistive AC circuits, the relationship between voltage and current, the calculation of instantaneous and average power, and the waveform properties.

Key Points

  • In a pure resistive AC circuit, voltage and current are in phase, expressed as $ v(t) = V_m \sin(\omega t) $ and $ i(t) = I_m \sin(\omega t) $.
  • The phase difference between voltage and current is 0 degrees.
  • Instantaneous power formula:

$$i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}$$

  • Average power is:

$$P = VI$$
which is the product of RMS voltage and RMS current.

  • The power waveform is always positive, indicating the resistor only consumes power and does not return power to the source.
  • Five characteristics of power in pure resistive AC circuit:
    1. Voltage and current phase difference is 0 degrees
    2. Average power $ P = VI \cos \theta = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R} $
    3. Maximum instantaneous power $ P_{max} = 2VI $
    4. Minimum instantaneous power $ P_{min} = 0 $
    5. Power frequency is twice the source frequency

純電阻交流電路與平均功率

本文說明純電阻交流電路的特性、電壓與電流的關係、瞬時功率與平均功率的計算，以及功率波形的特性。

重點歸納

  • 純電阻交流電路中，電壓與電流為同相位，皆為 $ v(t) = V_m \sin(\omega t) $ 和 $ i(t) = I_m \sin(\omega t) $。
  • 電壓與電流相位差為0度。
  • 瞬時功率公式為：

$$ i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

  • 平均功率為：

$$ P = VI$$
即電壓有效值乘以電流有效值。

  • 功率波形始終為正，表示純電阻只消耗功率，不會回饋電源。
  • 功率特性五大點：
    1. 電壓與電流相位差0度
    2. 平均功率 $ P = VI \cos \theta = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R} $
    3. 最大瞬時功率 $ P_{max} = 2VI $
    4. 最小瞬時功率 $ P_{min} = 0 $
    5. 功率頻率為電源頻率的兩倍

Example of AC Power Calculation in Pure Resistive Circuit

This article provides a detailed example of AC power calculation in a pure resistive circuit, including waveform, resistance, phase angle, and power formulas.

Key Points

  • Voltage waveform: $ v(t) = 10\sqrt{2} \sin(377t) $ volts
  • Resistance: 10 ohms
  • Phase angle $ \theta $ calculation:
    • Voltage and current phase angles are both 0 degrees
    • Phase difference $ \theta = 0^\circ $
  • Average power $ P $ calculation:
    • $ P = VI \cos \theta = 10 \times 1 \times \cos 0^\circ = 10 $ watts
  • Maximum instantaneous power $ P_{\max} $:
    • $ P_{\max} = 2VI = 2 \times 10 \times 1 = 20 $ watts
  • Minimum instantaneous power $ P_{\min} = 0 $ watts
