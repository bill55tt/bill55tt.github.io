Advanced Example Analysis of RL Discharge Transient Circuit

Overview

This comprehensive article provides an advanced example analysis of RL discharge transient circuits. We examine complex circuit configurations, apply superposition theorem, and demonstrate sophisticated problem-solving techniques for RL discharge phenomena.

Advanced RL Discharge Circuit Analysis

Circuit Configuration

Complex RL Discharge Circuit:

  • Inductor: L = 2 H (energy storage element)
  • Resistors: Multiple resistors in series-parallel configuration
  • Switch: Controls transition from steady state to discharge
  • Power Supply: Provides initial energy for charging
  • Multiple Paths: Complex discharge path analysis

Steady State Analysis

Before Switch Closure:

  • Inductor State: Fully charged, behaves as short circuit
  • Voltage Distribution:
    • Inductor voltage: 0V (steady state)
    • Resistor voltages: Distributed according to voltage division
  • Current: Calculated using total voltage and resistance
  • Energy State: Maximum magnetic energy stored

Current Calculation:
$$ I_{steady} = \frac{V_{total}}{R_{total}} = \frac{100V}{5k\Omega} = 20mA $$

Transient Analysis

1. Initial Discharge Conditions

When Switch S Closes:

  • Circuit Reconfiguration: New discharge path established
  • Inductor Behavior: Begins discharging stored energy
  • Initial Current: Maintains pre-closure value (20mA)
  • Voltage Polarity: Reverses compared to charging state

2. Voltage Analysis

Inductor Voltage Calculation:
$$ V_L(0) = I(0) \times R_{discharge} = 20mA \times 4k\Omega = 80V $$

Voltage Polarity:

  • Charging: Positive voltage across inductor
  • Discharging: Negative voltage across inductor (opposite polarity)

3. Time Constant Analysis

Discharge Time Constant:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{2H}{4k\Omega} = 0.5ms $$

Physical Interpretation:

  • Fast Discharge: Small time constant indicates rapid energy release
  • High Resistance: Large resistance in discharge path
  • Energy Dissipation: Rapid power transfer to resistors

Mathematical Analysis

1. Current Decay Formula

Exponential Decay Expression:
$$ i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = 20mA \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} $$

Formula Components:

  • ( i(t) )：Current at time t
  • ( I_0 )：Initial current (20mA)
  • ( t )：Time after discharge begins
  • ( τ )：Time constant (0.5ms)

2. Voltage Decay Formula

Inductor Voltage:
$$ V_L(t) = V_L(0) e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = 80V \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} $$

Resistor Voltage:
$$ V_R(t) = i(t) \times R = 20mA \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} \times 4k\Omega = 80V \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} $$

3. Time-Dependent Calculations

At t = 1ms (2τ):
$$ i(1ms) = 20mA \cdot e^{-2} = 20mA \cdot 0.135 = 2.7mA $$

$$ V_L(1ms) = 80V \cdot e^{-2} = 80V \cdot 0.135 = 10.8V $$

Superposition Theorem Application

1. Circuit Decomposition

Original Circuit: Complex RL discharge circuit
Decomposed Circuits:

  • Circuit A: Steady-state current source
  • Circuit B: Discharging inductor

2. Individual Circuit Analysis

Circuit A (Steady State):

  • Current Source: 20mA flowing through 4kΩ resistor
  • Voltage: ( V_A = 20mA \times 4k\Omega = 80V )

Circuit B (Discharge):

  • Inductor Current: Decaying from 20mA
  • Voltage: ( V_B(t) = 80V \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} )

3. Superposition Result

Switch S Current:
$$ I_S(t) = I_{steady} + i_{discharge}(t) = 20mA + 20mA \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{0.5ms}} $$

At t = 1ms:
$$ I_S(1ms) = 20mA + 2.7mA = 22.7mA $$

Total Current Through Switch:
$$ I_{total} = 100mA - 2.7mA = 97.3mA $$

Energy Considerations

1. Initial Magnetic Energy

Stored Energy:
$$ W_L = \frac{1}{2} L I_0^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 2H \times (20mA)^2 = 0.4mJ $$

2. Power Dissipation

Instantaneous Power:
$$ P_R(t) = i^2(t) R = (20mA)^2 \times 4k\Omega \times e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} = 1.6W \times e^{-\frac{2t}{0.5ms}} $$

3. Energy Transfer

Total Energy Dissipated:
$$ W_{total} = \int_0^{\infty} P_R(t) dt = 0.4mJ $$

Verification:
$$ W_{total} = \frac{1}{2} L I_0^2 = 0.4mJ $$

Analysis of Transient Current Formula in RL Discharge Circuit

Overview

This comprehensive article provides a detailed analysis of the transient current formula in RL discharge circuits. We examine the fundamental principles of inductor discharge, the mathematical relationships governing current decay, and the physical interpretation of the time constant in RL circuits.

RL Discharge Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

Basic RL Discharge Circuit:

  • Inductor: Stores magnetic energy during charging phase
  • Resistor: Provides discharge path for stored energy
  • Switch: Controls transition from charging to discharging state
  • Power Supply: Provides initial energy for charging (disconnected during discharge)

Switch Operation

Position 1 (Charging State):

  • Circuit State: Power supply connected to inductor
  • Inductor Behavior: Accumulates magnetic energy
  • Current: Builds up to steady-state value
  • Energy Storage: Maximum magnetic energy stored

Position 2 (Discharging State):

  • Circuit State: Power supply disconnected
  • Inductor Behavior: Releases stored magnetic energy
  • Current: Decays exponentially from initial value
  • Energy Transfer: From inductor to resistor

Transient Current Analysis

1. Initial Conditions

When Switch Moves from Position 1 to Position 2:

  • Power Supply: Removed from circuit
  • Inductor Current: Cannot change instantaneously (Lenz’s law)
  • Initial Current: ( i(0) = \frac{E}{R} )
  • Energy Conservation: Magnetic energy maintains current flow

2. Current Decay Characteristics

Exponential Decay Pattern:

  • Initial Value: ( i(0) = \frac{E}{R} ) (100% of steady-state current)
  • Decay Formula: ( i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} )
  • Final Value: ( i(\infty) = 0 ) (complete discharge)
  • Decay Rate: Determined by time constant τ

3. Time Constant Analysis

Mathematical Definition:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

Physical Interpretation:

  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Meaning: Time for current to decay to 36.8% of initial value
  • Circuit Response: Characterizes discharge rate
  • Energy Dissipation: Controls power transfer rate

Mathematical Derivation

1. Circuit Equation

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL):
$$ V_R(t) + V_L(t) = 0 $$

Component Voltages:

  • Resistor Voltage: ( V_R(t) = i(t) \times R )
  • Inductor Voltage: ( V_L(t) = L \frac{di(t)}{dt} )

Differential Equation:
$$ i(t)R + L \frac{di(t)}{dt} = 0 $$

2. Solution Method

Rearranging the Equation:
$$ L \frac{di(t)}{dt} = -i(t)R $$

$$ \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} dt $$

Integration:
$$ \int \frac{di(t)}{i(t)} = -\frac{R}{L} \int dt $$

$$ \ln i(t) = -\frac{R}{L} t + C $$

Exponential Form:
$$ i(t) = e^{-\frac{R}{L} t + C} = e^C \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Initial Condition Application:
$$ i(0) = \frac{E}{R} = e^C $$

Final Formula:
$$ i(t) = \frac{E}{R} e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Time-Dependent Behavior

Key Time Points

t = 0 (Discharge Start):

  • Current: ( i(0) = \frac{E}{R} ) (100% of initial current)
  • Energy: Maximum magnetic energy stored
  • Power: Maximum power dissipation in resistor

t = τ (One Time Constant):

  • Current: ( i(\tau) = 0.368 \frac{E}{R} ) (36.8% of initial current)
  • Energy: 63.2% of initial energy dissipated
  • Power: Reduced to 13.5% of initial power

t = 2τ (Two Time Constants):

  • Current: ( i(2\tau) = 0.135 \frac{E}{R} ) (13.5% of initial current)
  • Energy: 86.5% of initial energy dissipated
  • Power: Reduced to 1.8% of initial power

t = 3τ (Three Time Constants):

  • Current: ( i(3\tau) = 0.050 \frac{E}{R} ) (5.0% of initial current)
  • Energy: 95.0% of initial energy dissipated
  • Power: Reduced to 0.25% of initial power

t = 5τ (Five Time Constants):

  • Current: ( i(5\tau) \approx 0 ) (effectively zero)
  • Energy: 99.3% of initial energy dissipated
  • Power: Negligible power dissipation

Energy Considerations

1. Initial Magnetic Energy

Stored Energy:
$$ W_L = \frac{1}{2} L i^2(0) = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

2. Power Dissipation

Instantaneous Power:
$$ P_R(t) = i^2(t) R = \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 R e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} = \frac{E^2}{R} e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} $$

3. Total Energy Dissipated

Energy Conservation:
$$ W_{total} = \int_0^{\infty} P_R(t) dt = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

Verification:
$$ W_{total} = \frac{E^2}{R} \int_0^{\infty} e^{-\frac{2t}{\tau}} dt = \frac{E^2}{R} \cdot \frac{\tau}{2} = \frac{1}{2} L \left(\frac{E}{R}\right)^2 $$

  • Meaning: Time for current to decay to 36.8% of initial value
  • Circuit Behavior: Characterizes discharge rate

Resistor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the resistor during RL discharge is:

$$ V_R(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Formula Components:

  • ( V_R(t) )：Resistor voltage at time t
  • ( E )：Initial voltage (same as initial inductor voltage)
  • ( t )：Time after discharge begins
  • ( τ )：Time constant ( = \frac{L}{R} )

Physical Interpretation

Resistor Voltage Characteristics:

  • Initial Value: ( V_R(0) = E ) (maximum voltage)
  • Decay Pattern: Exponential decrease over time
  • Final Value: ( V_R(\infty) = 0 ) (zero voltage at steady state)
  • Energy Dissipation: Power dissipated as heat in resistor

Time-Dependent Behavior

  • t = 3τ: ( V_R(3\tau) = 0.050E ) (5.0% of initial voltage)
  • t = 5τ: ( V_R(5\tau) \approx 0 ) (effectively zero)

Inductor Voltage Analysis

Inductor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the inductor during RL discharge is:

$$ V_L(t) = -E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Formula Components:

  • ( V_L(t) )：Inductor voltage at time t
  • ( E )：Initial voltage magnitude
  • ( t )：Time after discharge begins
  • ( τ )：Time constant ( = \frac{L}{R} )
  • Negative Sign: Indicates opposite polarity to resistor voltage

Physical Interpretation

Inductor Voltage Characteristics:

  • Initial Value: ( V_L(0) = -E ) (negative of initial voltage)
  • Decay Pattern: Exponential decrease in magnitude
  • Final Value: ( V_L(\infty) = 0 ) (zero voltage at steady state)
  • Polarity: Opposite to resistor voltage to maintain current flow

Polarity Explanation

Why Negative Sign:

  • Lenz’s Law: Inductor opposes change in current
  • Current Direction: During discharge, current decreases
  • Voltage Polarity: Inductor generates voltage to oppose current decrease
  • Energy Conservation: Maintains circuit equilibrium

Voltage Relationship Analysis

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law Verification

At any time t, the sum of voltages around the loop must equal zero:

$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = 0 $$

Substituting Formulas:
$$ -E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = 0 $$

This confirms that our formulas satisfy KVL.

Voltage Magnitude Relationship

Equal Magnitudes:

  • Resistor Voltage: ( |V_R(t)| = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} )
  • Inductor Voltage: ( |V_L(t)| = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} )

Opposite Polarity:

  • Resistor: Positive voltage drop
  • Inductor: Negative voltage drop

Total Energy Dissipated

This confirms that all stored energy is dissipated in the resistor.

RL 放電電路基礎

電路配置

基本 RL 放電電路：

  • 電阻：放電期間消耗能量
  • 開關：控制放電開始
  • 初始條件：電感器攜帶初始電流

放電過程概述

當 RL 電路中的開關打開時：

  1. 能量轉移：儲存在電感器中的磁能轉移到電阻
  2. 電流衰減：電流隨時間指數衰減
  3. 電壓變化：電感和電阻電壓都遵循指數衰減
  4. 穩態：電路達到零電流和零電壓

數學基礎

時間常數定義

RL 電路的時間常數定義為：

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

物理解釋：

  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 意義：電流衰減到初始值 36.8% 的時間
  • 電路行為：表徵放電速率

電流衰減公式

RL 放電電路中的電流遵循：

$$ i(t) = I_0 e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

其中：

  • ( i(t) )：時間 t 時的電流
  • ( I_0 )：初始電流
  • ( t )：開關打開後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )

電阻電壓分析

電阻電壓公式

RL 放電期間電阻兩端的電壓為：

$$ V_R(t) = E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

公式組成：

  • ( V_R(t) )：時間 t 時的電阻電壓
  • ( E )：初始電壓（與初始電感電壓相同）
  • ( t )：放電開始後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )

物理解釋

電阻電壓特性：

  • 初始值：( V_R(0) = E )（最大電壓）
  • 衰減模式：隨時間指數衰減
  • 最終值：( V_R(\infty) = 0 )（穩態時零電壓）
  • 能量消耗：功率在電阻中以熱能形式消耗

時間相關行為

關鍵時間點：

  • t = 0：( V_R(0) = E )（初始電壓的 100%）
  • t = τ：( V_R(\tau) = 0.368E )（初始電壓的 36.8%）
  • t = 2τ：( V_R(2\tau) = 0.135E )（初始電壓的 13.5%）
  • t = 3τ：( V_R(3\tau) = 0.050E )（初始電壓的 5.0%）
  • t = 5τ：( V_R(5\tau) \approx 0 )（實質上為零）

電感電壓分析

電感電壓公式

RL 放電期間電感兩端的電壓為：

$$ V_L(t) = -E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

公式組成：

  • ( V_L(t) )：時間 t 時的電感電壓
  • ( E )：初始電壓大小
  • ( t )：放電開始後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )
  • 負號：表示與電阻電壓相反的極性

物理解釋

電感電壓特性：

  • 初始值：( V_L(0) = -E )（初始電壓的負值）
  • 衰減模式：大小指數衰減
  • 最終值：( V_L(\infty) = 0 )（穩態時零電壓）
  • 極性：與電阻電壓相反以維持電流流動

極性解釋

為什麼是負號：

  • 楞次定律：電感器反對電流變化
  • 電流方向：放電期間，電流減少
  • 電壓極性：電感器產生電壓以反對電流減少
  • 能量守恆：維持電路平衡

基爾霍夫電壓定律驗證

在任何時間 t，迴路中電壓總和必須等於零：

代入公式：

這確認了我們的公式滿足 KVL。

  • 關係：( |V_R(t)| = |V_L(t)| ) 在所有時間

相反極性：

  • 電阻：正電壓降
  • 電感：負電壓降
  • 淨效應：迴路中總電壓為零

RL Charging Transient Basic Formulas and Example Analysis

Overview

This comprehensive article provides detailed analysis of the fundamental formulas governing RL charging transient behavior and their application in practical example problems. We examine the mathematical relationships between current and voltage components, time constant calculations, and step-by-step problem-solving strategies.

RL Charging Circuit Fundamentals

Circuit Configuration

In a typical RL charging circuit:

  • Voltage Source: ( E ) (DC power supply)
  • Resistor: ( R ) (current limiting element)
  • Inductor: ( L ) (energy storage element)
  • Switch: Controls circuit operation
  • Time Constant: ( τ = \frac{L}{R} ) (characteristic charging time)

Charging Process Overview

The charging process involves:

  1. Initial State: Inductor acts as open circuit, no current flow
  2. Current Build-up: Current increases exponentially over time
  3. Voltage Distribution: Voltage gradually transfers from inductor to resistor
  4. Steady State: Current reaches maximum value ( \frac{E}{R} )

Fundamental Formulas

1. RL Charging Transient Current Formula

The current in an RL charging circuit follows the exponential growth function:

$$ i(t) = I_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

Where:

  • ( i(t) )：Current at time t
  • ( I_{\infty} )：Steady-state current ( = \frac{E}{R} )
  • ( t )：Time after switch closure
  • ( τ )：Time constant ( = \frac{L}{R} )

Physical Interpretation:

  • Current rises from 0 to steady-state value over time
  • Exponential growth pattern
  • Rate controlled by time constant

2. Resistor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the resistor follows the same exponential growth pattern:

$$ V_R(t) = V_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

Where:

  • ( V_R(t) )：Resistor voltage at time t
  • ( V_{\infty} )：Steady-state voltage ( = E )
  • ( t )：Time after switch closure
  • ( τ )：Time constant ( = \frac{L}{R} )

Physical Interpretation:

  • Resistor voltage rises from 0 to steady-state value
  • Follows same exponential pattern as current
  • Directly proportional to current (Ohm’s law)

3. Inductor Voltage Formula

The voltage across the inductor follows exponential decay:

$$ V_L(t) = V_0 \times e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

Where:

  • ( V_L(t) )：Inductor voltage at time t
  • ( V_0 )：Initial voltage ( = E )
  • ( t )：Time after switch closure
  • ( τ )：Time constant ( = \frac{L}{R} )

Physical Interpretation:

  • Inductor voltage decays from initial value to 0
  • Exponential decay pattern
  • Opposite behavior to resistor voltage

Time Constant Calculation

Definition

The time constant for RL circuits is:

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

Physical Significance

  • Units: Seconds (s)
  • Meaning: Time for current to reach 63.2% of final value
  • Growth Rate: Determines how quickly current builds up
  • Circuit Response: Characterizes the circuit’s transient behavior

Example Calculation

Given: ( L = 60mH ), ( R = 20Ω )

Time Constant:
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{60mH}{20Ω} = \frac{0.06H}{20Ω} = 0.003s = 3ms $$

Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law Verification

Circuit Equation

At any time t:
$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E $$

Formula Verification

Substituting the formulas:
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) = E $$

Simplifying:
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E - E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E $$

This confirms that the formulas satisfy Kirchhoff’s voltage law.

RL充電暫態基本公式與例題解析

概述

本文提供 RL 充電暫態行為基本公式的詳細分析及其在實際例題中的應用。我們檢視電流與電壓元件之間的數學關係、時間常數計算，以及逐步解題策略。

RL充電電路基礎

電路配置

典型的 RL 充電電路包含：

  • 電壓源：( E )（直流電源）
  • 電阻：( R )（限流元件）
  • 電感：( L )（儲能元件）
  • 開關：控制電路操作
  • 時間常數：( τ = \frac{L}{R} )（特徵充電時間）

充電過程概述

充電過程包含：

  1. 初始狀態：電感器呈斷路，無電流流動
  2. 電流建立：電流隨時間指數增長
  3. 電壓分佈：電壓逐漸從電感器轉移到電阻
  4. 穩態：電流達到最大值 ( \frac{E}{R} )

基本公式

1. RL充電暫態電流公式

RL 充電電路中的電流遵循指數增長函數：

$$ i(t) = I_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

其中：

  • ( i(t) )：時間 t 時的電流
  • ( I_{\infty} )：穩態電流 ( = \frac{E}{R} )
  • ( t )：開關閉合後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )

物理解釋：

  • 電流隨時間從 0 增加到穩態值
  • 指數增長模式
  • 速率由時間常數控制

2. 電阻電壓公式

電阻兩端電壓遵循相同的指數增長模式：

$$ V_R(t) = V_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) $$

其中：

  • ( V_R(t) )：時間 t 時的電阻電壓
  • ( V_{\infty} )：穩態電壓 ( = E )
  • ( t )：開關閉合後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )

物理解釋：

  • 電阻電壓從 0 增加到穩態值
  • 與電流遵循相同的指數模式
  • 與電流成正比（歐姆定律）

3. 電感電壓公式

電感兩端電壓遵循指數衰減：

$$ V_L(t) = V_0 \times e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} $$

其中：

  • ( V_L(t) )：時間 t 時的電感電壓
  • ( V_0 )：初始電壓 ( = E )
  • ( t )：開關閉合後的時間
  • ( τ )：時間常數 ( = \frac{L}{R} )

物理解釋：

  • 電感電壓從初始值衰減到 0
  • 指數衰減模式
  • 與電阻電壓相反的行為

時間常數計算

定義

RL 電路的時間常數為：

$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} $$

物理意義

  • 單位：秒（s）
  • 意義：電流達到最終值 63.2% 的時間
  • 增長率：決定電流建立的速度
  • 電路響應：表徵電路的暫態行為

計算範例

已知： ( L = 60mH )，( R = 20Ω )

時間常數：
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{60mH}{20Ω} = \frac{0.06H}{20Ω} = 0.003s = 3ms $$

基爾霍夫電壓定律驗證

電路方程式

在任何時間 t：
$$ V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E $$

公式驗證

代入公式：
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E(1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) = E $$

簡化：
$$ E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} + E - E \cdot e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} = E $$

這確認了公式滿足基爾霍夫電壓定律。

例題解析

問題設定

已知： ( E = 100V )，( R = 20Ω )，( L = 60mH )

時間常數：
$$ \tau = \frac{L}{R} = \frac{60mH}{20Ω} = 3ms $$

穩態值：

  • 電流：( I_{\infty} = \frac{E}{R} = \frac{100V}{20Ω} = 5A )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_{R\infty} = E = 100V )
  • 電感電壓：( V_{L\infty} = 0V )

不同時間點的計算

t = 0 時（開關閉合）

  • 電流：( i(0) = 5A \times (1 - e^0) = 0A )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_R(0) = 100V \times (1 - e^0) = 0V )
  • 電感電壓：( V_L(0) = 100V \times e^0 = 100V )

t = 3ms 時（一個時間常數）

  • 電流：( i(3ms) = 5A \times (1 - e^{-1}) = 3.16A )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_R(3ms) = 100V \times (1 - e^{-1}) = 63.2V )
  • 電感電壓：( V_L(3ms) = 100V \times e^{-1} = 36.8V )

t = 6ms 時（兩個時間常數）

  • 電流：( i(6ms) = 5A \times (1 - e^{-2}) = 4.33A )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_R(6ms) = 100V \times (1 - e^{-2}) = 86.5V )
  • 電感電壓：( V_L(6ms) = 100V \times e^{-2} = 13.5V )

t = 15ms 時（五個時間常數）

  • 電流：( i(15ms) = 5A \times (1 - e^{-5}) = 4.97A )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_R(15ms) = 100V \times (1 - e^{-5}) = 99.3V )
  • 電感電壓：( V_L(15ms) = 100V \times e^{-5} = 0.7V )

解題策略

1. 識別參數

  • 從問題中提取 E、R、L 值
  • 計算時間常數 ( \tau = \frac{L}{R} )
  • 確定穩態值
  • 識別感興趣的時間點

2. 應用基本公式

  • 電流：( i(t) = I_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) )
  • 電阻電壓：( V_R(t) = V_{\infty} \times (1 - e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}) )
  • 電感電壓：( V_L(t) = V_0 \times e^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} )

3. 驗證結果

  • 檢查單位（電流為安培，電壓為伏特）
  • 確保基爾霍夫電壓定律得到滿足
  • 驗證物理合理性
  • 交叉檢查計算

  • 驗證基爾霍夫電壓定律：( V_L(t) + V_R(t) = E )

  • 事實：電流遵循指數增長模式

2. 瞬間電流流動

  • 誤解：開關閉合時電流立即流動
  • 事實：由於電感器反對，電流逐漸建立

  • 事實：增長率隨時間指數衰減

  • 誤解：所有能量在電阻中損失
  • 事實：能量儲存在電感器的磁場中
