使用阻抗觀念求功率因數（PF）— R-L 串聯與並聯電路 / Calculating PF Using Impedance — R-L Series & Parallel

Posted on 2025-08-21 Edited on 2026-02-20 In AC Circuits Views: Disqus:

English Version

Title

Calculating Power Factor (PF) Using Impedance Concept — R-L Series and Parallel Circuits

Introduction

  • Scope: Derive PF from impedance when phasors are not available, for RL series and parallel.
  • Why it matters: PF can be inferred from circuit parameters for quick estimation and design checks.
  • What you’ll learn: PF via $Z$: series PF = $R/|Z|$; parallel PF via $|Z|$ relation.

Key Points Summary

  • Methods to compute PF:
    • From phase angle $\theta$: $ PF = \cos\theta $
    • From powers: $ PF = P/S $
  • Using impedance when $\bar{V}$, $\bar{I}$ are unavailable.
  • RL series circuit:
    • $ |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} $
    • $ PF = \frac{R}{\sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2}} $
  • RL parallel circuit:
    • From equivalent $|Z|$: $ PF = \frac{|Z|}{R} $
    • Using the provided relation: $ PF = \frac{\sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2}}{X_L} $
  • Series vs. parallel PF differ; see Part G for details.

Reference

中文版

標題

使用阻抗觀念求功率因數（PF）— R-L 串聯與並聯電路

前言

  • 主題範圍：在無法直接取得相量時，利用阻抗推導 RL 串聯與並聯電路的 PF。
  • 為何重要：可由電路參數快速估算 PF，利於設計與評估。
  • 你將學到：由 $Z$ 出發的 PF 公式：串聯 $PF = R/|Z|$，並聯由 $|Z|$ 推得。

重點統整

  • PF 計算方式：
    • 由相位角 $\theta$：$ PF = \cos\theta $
    • 由功率比：$ PF = P/S $
  • 相量不可得時，改用阻抗求 PF。
  • R-L 串聯電路：
    • $ |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2} $
    • $ PF = \frac{R}{\sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2}} $
  • R-L 並聯電路：
    • 先求等效 $|Z|$，再得 $ PF = \frac{|Z|}{R} $
    • 依提供關係：$ PF = \frac{\sqrt{R^2 + X_L^2}}{X_L} $
  • 串聯與並聯之 PF 表達不同，詳見 Part G。

High School Basic Electricity_AC Power_Apparent Power_PART A Apparent Power_Prof. Chen Cheng-Hsu

This article explains the concepts of apparent power, average power, and reactive power in AC circuits, including their formulas, physical meanings, and real-life analogies.

Key Points (English)

  • Apparent Power (S): The product of voltage $V$ and current $I$, representing the total power in the circuit. Unit: volt-ampere (VA).

  • Average Power (P): The real power consumed by a pure resistive load, analogous to rent—actual energy spent.

  • Reactive Power (Q): The power stored and released by inductive and capacitive loads, analogous to a deposit—eventually returned.

  • Meaning of Apparent Power:

    • At the source: Total power delivered to the load.
    • At the load: Rated capacity and rated current of electrical equipment.

  • Apparent Power Formula:

  • 視在功率的計算公式：

$$S = VI = I^2 Z = \frac{V^2}{Z}$$

或是利用功率三角形關係：

$$S = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2}$$

單位為 伏安 (VA)。
Unit: volt-ampere (VA).

  • English Terms:
    • Apparent Power: 視在功率
    • Average Power (or Real Power): 平均功率
    • Reactive Power: 虛功率
  • Real-life Analogy:
    • Rent (Average Power): Actual expense.
    • Deposit (Reactive Power): Temporarily paid, eventually returned.
    • Apparent Power: The sum of rent and deposit, representing the total amount you need to prepare.

本文說明交流電路中視在功率、平均功率與虛功率的定義、公式、物理意義與生活化比喻。

重點歸納（中文）

  • 視在功率 (Apparent Power)：電壓 $V$ 與電流 $I$ 的乘積，表示電路中電壓與電流的總功率，單位為伏安 (VA)。
  • 平均功率 (Average Power)：純電阻負載消耗的實際功率，類比為租房的租金，是實際花費的能量。
  • 虛功率 (Reactive Power)：電感與電容負載儲存並釋放能量的功率，類比為押金，最後會退還。
  • 視在功率的意義：
    • 電源端：表示電源輸送給負載的總功率。
    • 負載端：表示電力設備的額定容量與額定電流。
  • 視在功率的計算公式：

$$S = VI = I^2 Z = \frac{V^2}{Z}$$

或是利用功率三角形關係：

$$S = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2}$$

單位為 伏安 (VA)。

  • 英文翻譯：
    • 視在功率：Apparent Power
    • 平均功率：Average Power (或 Real Power)
    • 虛功率：Reactive Power
  • 生活化比喻：
    • 租金（平均功率）是實際花費。
    • 押金（虛功率）是暫時繳納，最後會退還。
    • 視在功率是租金加押金的總和，代表你必須準備的總金額。

High School Basic Electricity_AC Power_Average Power_PART A Definition and Example of Average Power Calculation_Prof. Chen Cheng-Hsu

Posted on 2025-08-03 Edited on 2026-03-02 In AC Circuits Views: Disqus:

High School Basic Electricity_AC Power_Average Power_PART A Definition and Example of Average Power Calculation_Prof. Chen Cheng-Hsu

This article explains the definition of average power in AC circuits, the calculation formula, the effect of phase difference, and provides a detailed example.

Key Points

  • Definition of Average Power: The average power is the mean value of the instantaneous power $ p(t) $ over a complete cycle.
  • Voltage and Current Waveforms:
    • Voltage $ v(t) = V_m \sin(\omega t) $ with phase angle set to 0° for simplicity.
    • Current $ i(t) = I_m \sin(\omega t - \theta) $.
  • Instantaneous Power Formula:

$$p(t) = v(t) \times i(t) = VI \cos \theta - VI \cos(2\omega t - \theta)$$

  • Average Power Calculation:
    • $ VI \cos \theta $ is a constant, product of RMS values and cosine of phase angle.
    • $ VI \cos(2\omega t - \theta) $ is a symmetric sinusoid with zero average.
    • Therefore, average power $ P = VI \cos \theta $.
  • Effect of Phase Difference: If voltage and current have phase angles $ \theta_v $ and $ \theta_i $, then

$$P = VI \cos(\theta_v - \theta_i)$$

  • Example Calculation:
    • Voltage: $ v(t) = 50\sqrt{2} \sin(377t + 30^\circ) $, RMS $ V = 50 $, phase angle $ \theta_v = 30^\circ $.
    • Current: $ i(t) = 5\sqrt{2} \sin(377t - 30^\circ) $, RMS $ I = 5 $, phase angle $ \theta_i = -30^\circ $.
    • Average power:

$$P = VI \cos(\theta_v - \theta_i)$$

高中基本電學_交流電功率_平均功率_PART A 平均功率定義、範例：平均功率計算_陳政旭

本文說明交流電平均功率的定義、計算公式、相位差影響，並提供詳細範例。

重點歸納

  • 平均功率定義：平均功率是完整週期內瞬間功率 $ p(t) $ 的平均值。
  • 電壓與電流波形：
    • 電壓 $ v(t) = V_m \sin(\omega t) $，相位角設為0度方便說明。
    • 電流 $ i(t) = I_m \sin(\omega t - \theta) $。
  • 瞬間功率公式：

$$p(t) = v(t) \times i(t) = VI \cos \theta - VI \cos(2\omega t - \theta)$$

  • 平均功率計算：
    • $ VI \cos \theta $ 是常數，為有效值乘上相位角的餘弦。
    • $ VI \cos(2\omega t - \theta) $ 是正負對稱的正弦波，平均值為0。
    • 因此平均功率 $ P = VI \cos \theta $。
  • 相位差影響：若電壓與電流相位角分別為 $ \theta_v $ 與 $ \theta_i $，則

$$P = VI \cos(\theta_v - \theta_i)$$

  • 範例計算：
    • 電壓：$ v(t) = 50\sqrt{2} \sin(377t + 30^\circ) $，有效值 $ V = 50 $，相位角 $ \theta_v = 30^\circ $。
    • 電流：$ i(t) = 5\sqrt{2} \sin(377t - 30^\circ) $，有效值 $ I = 5 $，相位角 $ \theta_i = -30^\circ $。
    • 平均功率：

$$P = 50 \times 5 \times \cos (30^\circ - (-30^\circ)) = 250 \times \cos 60^\circ = 125 \text {瓦}$$

Example of Instantaneous Power in AC Circuits

This article analyzes an example of instantaneous power calculation in AC circuits, including waveform equations, RMS values, frequency, and the physical meaning of negative instantaneous power.

Key Points

  • Instantaneous power $p(t)$ is the product of voltage $v(t)$ and current $i(t)$.
  • Voltage and current waveforms:

$$v(t) = 10\sqrt{2} \sin(314t) , V$$

$$i(t) = 2\sqrt{2} \sin(314t - 60^\circ) , A$$

  • Instantaneous power formula uses trigonometric product identities:

$$p(t) = VI \cos(\theta_v - \theta_i) - VI \cos(\theta_v + \theta_i)$$

  • RMS values:
    • Voltage RMS $V = 10,V$
    • Current RMS $I = 2,A$
  • Frequency calculation:

$$\omega = 2\pi f_p = 628 \Rightarrow f_p = 100,Hz$$

  • Maximum and minimum instantaneous power:

$$P_{\max} = 30,W, \quad P_{\min} = -10,W$$

  • Negative instantaneous power means the load returns power to the source.

交流電功率瞬間功率範例解析

本文解析交流電路中瞬間功率的計算範例，包含波形方程式、有效值、頻率計算，以及負瞬間功率的物理意義。

重點歸納

  • 瞬間功率 $p(t)$ 計算為電壓 $v(t)$ 與電流 $i(t)$ 的乘積。
  • 電壓與電流波形分別為：

$$v (t) = 10\sqrt {2} \sin (314t) \quad \text {伏特}$$

$$i (t) = 2\sqrt {2} \sin (314t - 60^\circ) \quad \text {安培}$$

  • 瞬間功率公式利用三角函數乘積轉換：

$$p(t) = VI \cos(\theta_v - \theta_i) - VI \cos(\theta_v + \theta_i)$$

  • 有效值計算：
    • 電壓有效值 $V = 10,V$
    • 電流有效值 $I = 2,A$
  • 頻率計算：

$$\omega = 2\pi f_p = 628 \Rightarrow f_p = \frac{628}{2\pi} = 100,Hz$$

  • 瞬間功率最大值與最小值：

$$P_{\max} = 10 + 20 = 30,W$$

$$P_{\min} = 10 - 20 = -10,W$$

  • 負瞬間功率代表負載將功率回饋給電源。

AC Power Reactive Power Part F Examples

This article provides comprehensive examples of average power and reactive power calculations in various AC circuits, including pure capacitive, RL series, and parallel RC circuits.

Key Points Summary (English)

  • Example 1: Pure Capacitive Circuit

    • Voltage $ v(t) = 100\sqrt{2} \sin(500t) $
    • Capacitive Reactance $ X_C = 50 \Omega $
    • Reactive Power $ Q_C = \frac{V^2}{X_C} = \frac{100^2}{50} = 200 $ VAR

  • Example 2: RL Series Circuit

    • Voltage $ v(t) = 200\sqrt{2} \sin(377t) $
    • Resistance $ R = 12 \Omega $, Inductive Reactance $ X_L = 16 \Omega $
    • Total Impedance $ Z = 12 + j16 = 20 \angle 53^\circ $
    • Current $ I = \frac{V}{Z} = 10 \angle -53^\circ $ A
    • Average Power $ P = I^2 R = 10^2 \times 12 = 1200 $ W
    • Reactive Power $ Q = I^2 X_L = 10^2 \times 16 = 1600 $ VAR

  • Example 3: Parallel Resistor and Capacitor Circuit

    • Voltage $ V = 40 \angle 0^\circ $ V
    • Resistance $ R = 20 \Omega $, Capacitive Reactance $ X_C = 80 \Omega $
    • Average Power $ P = \frac{V^2}{R} = \frac{40^2}{20} = 80 $ W
    • Reactive Power $ Q = \frac{V^2}{X_C} = \frac{40^2}{80} = 20 $ VAR
