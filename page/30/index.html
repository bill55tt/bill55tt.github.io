2-5.10 RC Charging Circuit Transient Formula Review

Key Points

Transient Changes

  • In RC charging circuit:
    • Resistor voltage changes
    • Capacitor voltage changes
    • Circuit current changes
  • All changes are time-dependent

Mathematical Description

  • Changes described by formulas
  • Natural exponential function: $ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $
  • Time constant: $ \tau = RC $

Voltage and Current Formulas

  • Capacitor voltage $ V_C(t) $:

    • Increases over time
    • Formula: $ 1 - e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

  • Resistor voltage $ V_R(t) $:

    • Decreases over time
    • Formula: $ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

  • Circuit current $ i(t) $:

    • Decreases over time
    • Formula: $ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

Time Characteristics

  • Time constant $ \tau = RC $
  • Transient disappears at $ 5\tau $
  • Circuit reaches steady state

2-5.10 RC充電電路暫態公式複習

重點歸納

暫態變化

  • RC充電電路中：
    • 電阻兩端電壓變化
    • 電容兩端電壓變化
    • 迴路電流變化
  • 所有變化都與時間相關

數學描述

  • 變化可用數學公式描述
  • 自然指數函數：$ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $
  • 時間常數：$ \tau = RC $

電壓與電流公式

  • 電容電壓 $ V_C(t) $：

    • 隨時間增加
    • 公式：$ 1 - e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

  • 電阻電壓 $ V_R(t) $：

    • 隨時間減少
    • 公式：$ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

  • 迴路電流 $ i(t) $：

    • 隨時間減少
    • 公式：$ e^{-\frac{t}{RC}} $

時間特性

  • 時間常數 $ \tau = RC $
  • 約在 $ 5\tau $ 時暫態消失
  • 電路進入穩定狀態

2-5.1 Resistance Temperature Coefficient Examples

Key Points

  1. Definition of Temperature Coefficient of Resistance (TCR)
    The TCR (α) represents the fractional change in resistance per degree Celsius increase in temperature, calculated by:

$$\alpha_{t1} = \frac{R_2 - R_1}{(t_2 - t_1) R_1}$$

where $R_1$ and $R_2$ are the resistances at temperatures $t_1$ and $t_2$ respectively.

  1. Example Calculations
    • At 20°C, resistance is 2Ω; at 220°C, resistance is 4Ω; TCR at 20°C is 0.005.
    • Resistance at other temperatures can be found by:

$$R_2 = R_1 \times [1 + \alpha_{t1} (t_2 - t_1)]$$

For example, resistance at 200°C is 3.8Ω.

  1. Positive Temperature Coefficient
    Resistance increases with temperature, known as positive temperature coefficient.

  2. TCR Variation at High Temperatures
    Use a modified formula to calculate TCR at high temperatures (e.g., 2500°C).

  3. Copper Wire Resistance Calculation
    Calculate resistance at different temperatures using copper’s absolute temperature property.

2-5.1 電阻溫度係數範例練習

重點歸納

  1. 電阻溫度係數定義
    電阻溫度係數（α）表示溫度每升高1°C時，電阻值增加的比例，計算公式為：

$$\alpha_{t1} = \frac{R_2 - R_1}{(t_2 - t_1) R_1}$$

其中，$R_1$和$R_2$分別是溫度$t_1$和$t_2$時的電阻值。

  1. 範例計算

    • 例題中，20°C時電阻為2Ω，220°C時電阻為4Ω，計算20°C時的電阻溫度係數為0.005。
    • 利用電阻溫度係數計算其他溫度下的電阻值，公式為：

    $$R_2 = R_1 \times [1 + \alpha_{t1} (t_2 - t_1)]$$

    例如，計算200°C時的電阻值為3.8Ω。

  2. 正溫度係數特性
    電阻隨溫度升高而增加，稱為正溫度係數。

  3. 高溫下電阻溫度係數變化
    利用變形公式計算高溫（如2500°C）時的電阻溫度係數。

  4. 銅線電阻計算
    利用銅的絕對溫度特性計算不同溫度下的電阻值。

2-4.3 Series and Parallel Circuits / 2-4.3 串並聯電路與電路組成基本型態

Posted on 2024-05-24 Edited on 2026-02-20 In DC Circuits Views: Disqus:

2-4.3 Series and Parallel Circuits

Key Points Summary

  • Basic Circuit Components:

    • Power source: supplies energy
    • Wires: medium to transfer energy
    • Load: consumes energy, mainly resistive loads (inductive and capacitive loads introduced later)

  • Current Flow Conditions:

    • Closed circuit (conducting path): energy flows normally
    • Open circuit: switch open or load damaged, no current flow
    • Short circuit: load terminals connected by a short wire, current bypasses load

  • Example Problems:

    • Example 1: Calculate current in closed circuit using Ohm’s law $ I = \frac{V}{R} $
    • Example 2: Current is zero in open circuit
    • Example 3: Large current in short circuit, no current through load

  • Overcurrent Protector:

    • Disconnects circuit when current is too high to prevent wire overheating and fire

2-4.3 串並聯電路與電路組成基本型態

重點歸納

  • 電路基本組成：

    • 電源：提供能量
    • 導線：傳遞能量的媒介
    • 負載：消耗能量，主要以電阻性負載為主（後續章節介紹電感性與電容性負載）

  • 電流流通情形：

    • 通路：形成封閉迴路，能量正常傳遞
    • 斷路：開關開啟或負載燒毀，電流無法通過
    • 短路：負載兩端被短路線接通，電流走無阻路徑，負載無電流通過

  • 例題說明：

    • 例題一：通路中電流計算，使用歐姆定律 $ I = \frac{V}{R} $
    • 例題二：斷路時電流為零
    • 例題三：短路時電流大，負載無電流

  • 過電流保護器：

    • 當電流過大時，保護器會斷開電路，防止導線過熱走火

Conversion Between Voltage Source and Current Source / 電壓源與電流源的互換

Posted on 2024-05-24 Edited on 2026-03-07 In DC Circuits Views: Disqus:

Conversion Between Voltage Source and Current Source

Key Points

  • Voltage source model: voltage $ E $ in series with internal resistance $ r $
  • Current source model: current $ I $ in parallel with internal resistance $ r $
  • Equivalence condition: internal resistance $ r $ is the same
  • Conversion formulas:

$$I = \frac{E}{r}, \quad E = I \times r$$

  • Ideal voltage source has zero internal resistance; ideal current source has infinite internal resistance; they cannot be converted in these ideal cases
  • Example calculations:
    • Voltage source to current source: $ I = \frac{E}{r} $
    • Current source to voltage source: $ E = I \times r $
  • Conversion helps simplify circuit analysis in practical applications

電壓源與電流源的互換

重點歸納

  • 電壓源模型：外加電壓 $ E $ 串聯內電阻 $ r $
  • 電流源模型：電流 $ I $ 並聯內電阻 $ r $
  • 兩者等效條件：內阻 $ r $ 相等
  • 互換關係：

$$I = \frac{E}{r}, \quad E = I \times r$$

  • 理想電壓源內阻為0，理想電流源內阻趨近無限大，無法互換
  • 範例計算：
    • 電壓源轉電流源：$ I = \frac{E}{r} $
    • 電流源轉電壓源：$ E = I \times r $
  • 實際應用中可用互換簡化電路計算
