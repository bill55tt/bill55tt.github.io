Basic Introduction to Voltage and Current Sources

Key Points

  • Power Source Classification: Divided into voltage sources and current sources, which can be equivalently transformed
  • Real Voltage Source: Composed of a fixed electromotive force $E$ in series with an internal resistance $r$
  • Ideal Voltage Source: Has zero internal resistance $r=0$, providing a constant output voltage regardless of load current
  • Load Effect: When connected to a load, terminal voltage $V_L = E - Ir$, current $I = \frac{E}{r + R_L}$
  • Internal Resistance Effect: The smaller the internal resistance, the closer the terminal voltage is to $E$, stabilizing the load voltage
  • Example Calculations:
    • Open-circuit load: terminal voltage equals electromotive force $E$
    • Load of 20 ohms: calculate current and terminal voltage, demonstrating voltage drop across internal resistance
  • Parameter Back-Calculation: Use different load and terminal voltage data to solve for $E$ and $r$
  • Conclusion: An ideal voltage source should have low internal resistance to maintain stable load voltage

電壓源及電流源基礎介紹

重點歸納

  • 電源分類：分為電壓源與電流源，兩者可互相等效轉換
  • 實際電壓源：由固定電動勢 $E$ 與內部電阻 $r$ 串聯組成
  • 理想電壓源：內阻 $r=0$，輸出電壓固定不變，與負載電流無關
  • 負載影響：連接負載時，端電壓 $V_L = E - Ir$，電流 $I = \frac{E}{r + R_L}$
  • 內阻影響：內阻越小，端電壓越接近電動勢 $E$，負載端電壓越穩定
  • 範例計算：
    • 負載為開路時，端電壓等於電動勢 $E$
    • 負載為20歐姆時，計算電流與端電壓，示範內阻電壓降
  • 反求電壓源參數：透過不同負載與端電壓數據反推 $E$ 與 $r$
  • 結論：理想電壓源應具備低內阻特性，確保負載端電壓穩定

Ideal and Practical Voltage Sources

Key Points

  • Ideal Voltage Source: Internal resistance $ r = 0 $, provides a fixed voltage at the load regardless of current changes
  • Load Voltage: Load voltage $ V_L $ always equals the source voltage $ E $
  • Practical Example: A 1.5V dry cell maintains 1.5V across different loads
  • Load Resistance Effect: Changing load resistance $ R_L $ adjusts current $ I $, voltage remains constant
  • Example Calculations:
    • $ V_{ab} = 12\text{ V} $ (ideal voltage source voltage)
    • $ I_1 = \frac{12\text{ V}}{6\Omega} = 2\text{ A} $
    • $ V_1 = 12\text{ V} - 4\text{ V} = 8\text{ V} $
    • $ I_2 = \frac{8\text{ V}}{2\Omega} = 4\text{ A} $

理想電壓源與實際電壓源

重點歸納

  • 理想電壓源：內阻 $ r = 0 $，提供負載端固定電壓，不受電流變化影響
  • 負載端電壓：負載端電壓 $ V_L $ 始終等於電壓源電壓 $ E $
  • 實際例子：1.5V 乾電池，接不同負載，電壓仍維持1.5V
  • 負載電阻影響：調整負載電阻 $ R_L $ 可改變電流 $ I $，電壓不變
  • 範例計算：
    • $ V_{ab} = 12\text{ V} $（理想電壓源電壓）
    • $ I_1 = \frac{12\text{ V}}{6\Omega} = 2\text{ A} $
    • $ V_1 = 12\text{ V} - 4\text{ V} = 8\text{ V} $
    • $ I_2 = \frac{8\text{ V}}{2\Omega} = 4\text{ A} $

Key Points Summary and English Translation of Wheatstone Bridge Examples

Key Points

  • Wheatstone bridge is used to measure unknown resistor $ R_s $.
  • Bridge balance condition: galvanometer reads zero, meaning the product of opposite resistors are equal.
  • At balance, use the formula $ R_1 \times R_x = R_2 \times R_3 $ to find the unknown resistor.
  • The middle resistor can be considered open circuit at balance, simplifying to series-parallel circuits.
  • Example calculates unknown resistor $ R_s = 3 \Omega $.
  • Use balance condition to identify bridge structure and calculate terminal resistance $ R_{ab} $.

惠斯登電橋範例重點歸納與英文翻譯

重點歸納

  • 惠斯登電橋用來測量未知電阻Rs。
  • 電橋平衡條件：檢流計指示為0，表示對角電阻乘積相等。
  • 平衡時公式為：$ R_1 \times R_x = R_2 \times R_3 $，可求未知電阻。
  • 平衡時中間電阻可視為開路，化簡為串並聯電路。
  • 範例中求得未知電阻Rs為3歐姆。
  • 利用平衡條件判斷電橋結構，計算端點電阻Rab。

Introduction and Application Highlights of the Wheatstone Bridge / 惠斯登電橋介紹與應用重點

Posted on 2024-05-24 Edited on 2026-03-07 In DC Circuits Views: Disqus:

Introduction and Application Highlights of the Wheatstone Bridge

Key Points

  • Function: Used to measure an unknown resistor (RX).
  • Main Components:
    • RX: Unknown resistor
    • Rs: Variable resistor for adjustment
    • G: Galvanometer (current meter)
  • Bridge Balance Condition: The bridge is balanced when the galvanometer shows zero current.
  • Resistance Relationship at Balance:

$$R_X = \frac{R_2}{R_1} \times R_s$$

  • Equivalent Circuit: At balance, the galvanometer current is zero, voltages at two points are equal, and the middle bridge is effectively open.
  • Application: By adjusting Rs to balance the bridge, the unknown resistance can be precisely measured.

Reference Video

YouTube Video: Introduction to Wheatstone Bridge

惠斯登電橋介紹與應用重點

重點歸納

  • 惠斯登電橋功能：用於測量未知電阻值（RX）。
  • 主要元件：
    • RX：待測電阻
    • Rs：可變電阻，用於調整
    • G：檢流計（電流表）
  • 電橋平衡條件：當檢流計指示電流為0時，電橋達到平衡。
  • 平衡時的電阻關係式：

$$R_X = \frac{R_2}{R_1} \times R_s$$

  • 等效電路：平衡時檢流計電流為0，兩點電壓相等，電橋中間點可視為開路。
  • 應用：透過調整Rs使電橋平衡，精確測量未知電阻。
