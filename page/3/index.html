Power Characteristics of Pure Capacitive AC Circuit

Power Characteristics of Pure Capacitive AC Circuit

This article summarizes the power characteristics of pure capacitive AC circuits, including phase difference, average power, reactive power, and waveform properties.

Key Points Summary (English)

  1. Voltage and current in a pure capacitive AC circuit have a phase difference of -90 degrees, with current leading voltage by 90 degrees.
  2. Average power is zero because positive and negative power waves cancel out; pure capacitors do not consume average power.
  3. Reactive power $Q_c$ formulas:
    • $Q_c = VI = I^2 X_c = \frac{V^2}{X_c}$
  4. Instantaneous maximum power $P_{max} = VI$ and minimum power $P_{min} = -VI$**.
  5. Power frequency is twice the source frequency.
  6. Power waveforms of pure inductors and capacitors differ by 180 degrees, being opposite in phase.
  7. When inductors and capacitors coexist in a circuit, their reactive powers subtract.

純電容交流電路之功率特性

本文整理純電容交流電路的功率特性，包括相位差、平均功率、虛功率與波形特性。

重點歸納（中文）

  1. 純電容交流電路的電壓與電流相位差為 -90度，電流超前電壓90度。
  2. 平均功率為零，因為正負功率波形相互抵銷，純電容不消耗平均功率。
  3. 虛功率 $Q_c$ 計算公式：
    • $Q_c = VI = I^2 X_c = \frac{V^2}{X_c}$
  4. 瞬間最大功率 $P_{max} = VI$，瞬間最小功率 $P_{min} = -VI$。
  5. 功率頻率為電源頻率的兩倍。
  6. 純電感與純電容功率波形相差180度，互為反向。
  7. 電路中同時有電感與電容時，虛功率做相減運算。

Title

Comprehensive Practice of AC Circuit Power (Part E Example)

Introduction

  • Scope: Use given time-domain waveforms to construct phasors and compute PF, S, P, and Q.
  • Why it matters: Connect time-domain signals to phasor-domain analysis for power calculations.
  • What you’ll learn: Convert cos to sin with a phase shift, identify load type, and compute power quantities.

Key Points

  • Given:
    • $ v(t) = 200\sqrt{2} \cos(500t) $
    • $ i(t) = 20\sqrt{2} \sin(500t) $
  • Phase relation:
    • $ v(t) = 200\sqrt{2} \sin(500t + 90^\circ) $
    • Voltage phasor: $ 200 \angle 90^\circ $
    • Current phasor: $ 20 \angle 0^\circ $
    • $ \Delta\theta = 90^\circ $ → current lags 90° → pure inductive load.
  • Load type and value:
    • Pure inductive
    • $ X_L = V/I = 10,\Omega $
    • $ L = X_L/\omega = 10/500 = 20,\text{mH} $
  • PF: $ \cos 90^\circ = 0 $ → PF = 0 (lagging)
  • Apparent power: $ S = V I = 200 \times 20 = 4000 $ VA
  • Active power: $ P = VI\cos\theta = 0 $
  • Reactive power: $ Q = VI\sin\theta = 4000 $ var

高中基本電學_交流電功率_PART B 範例：PT/QT/ST 與 PF（電路圖題型一）

Posted on 2025-08-03 Edited on 2026-02-20 In AC Circuits Views: Disqus:

English Version

Title

Basic Electrical Engineering – AC Power – PART B Example: PT, QT, ST, and PF (Circuit Diagram Type 1)

Introduction

  • Scope: Compute total active, reactive, and apparent power plus PF from a multi-branch circuit.
  • Why it matters: Aggregating branch P and Q correctly is essential to get the overall PF right.
  • What you’ll learn: Branch RMS currents, branch P/Q, totals PT/QT, and ST, PF derivation.

Key Points Summary

  • Problem type: Circuit diagram Type 1 → find PT, QT, ST, PF.
  • Currents:
    • Source RMS voltage = 200 V.
    • Each branch impedance = 20 Ω → $ I_1 = I_2 = I_3 = 10 $ A (RMS).
  • Total Active Power $P_T$:
    • $ P_1 = I_1^2 \times 20 = 10^2 \times 20 = 2000 $ W
    • $ P_2 = I_2^2 \times 16 = 10^2 \times 16 = 1600 $ W
    • $ P_3 = I_3^2 \times 16 = 10^2 \times 16 = 1600 $ W
    • $ P_T = 2000 + 1600 + 1600 = 5200 $ W
  • Total Reactive Power $Q_T$:
    • Inductive: $ Q_L = I_2^2 \times 12 = 10^2 \times 12 = 1200 $ var
    • Capacitive: $ Q_C = I_3^2 \times 12 = 10^2 \times 12 = 1200 $ var
    • $ Q_T = |Q_L - Q_C| = 0 $ var
  • Total Apparent Power $S_T$:
    • $ S_T = \sqrt{P_T^2 + Q_T^2} = \sqrt{5200^2 + 0} = 5200 $ VA
  • Power Factor $PF$:
    • $ PF = P_T / S_T = 5200/5200 = 1 $

高中基本電學_交流電功率_功率因數之改善_PART A 為何要改善功率因數？

Posted on 2025-08-03 Edited on 2026-02-20 In AC Circuits Views: Disqus:

English Version

Title

High School Basic Electricity – AC Power – Power Factor Improvement – PART A: Why Improve Power Factor?

Introduction

  • Scope: Explain the meaning of power factor (PF), the downsides of low PF, and benefits of improvement.
  • Why it matters: PF impacts losses, voltage regulation, and equipment sizing; utilities also price by PF.
  • What you’ll learn: Practical reasons and typical outcomes of PF correction in distribution systems.

Key Points Summary

  • Meaning of PF: PF = active power / apparent power; PF 71% → only 71% useful, 29% reactive.
  • Effects of low PF:
    • Higher input current → more copper, higher line losses ($I^2R$).
    • Larger voltage drop → degraded power quality.
    • Bigger capacity required for transformers/generators.
  • Benefits of improving PF:
    • Save electricity bills (penalized < 80%, discounts > 80% vary by utility).
    • Reduce input current, increasing line/transformer capacity.
    • Improve power quality; stabilize voltage.
    • Extend equipment lifespan.

Reference

  • YouTube: Power Factor Improvement – PART A

中文版

標題

高中基本電學_交流電功率_功率因數之改善_PART A 為何要改善功率因數？

前言

  • 主題範圍：說明功率因數（PF）的意義、低 PF 的不利影響，以及改善之效益。
  • 為何重要：PF 影響線路損失、電壓調節與設備容量，亦與電費計價相關。
  • 你將學到：配電系統進行 PF 改善的實務原因與常見成效。

重點統整

  • 功率因數意義：PF = 有效功率 / 視在功率；PF 71% → 有效利用 71%，29% 為虛功。
  • PF 低的影響：
    • 輸入電流提高 → 導線銅量增加、線路損失（$I^2R$）變大。
    • 線路壓降上升 → 供電品質變差。
    • 變壓器、發電設備所需容量提高。
  • 改善 PF 的好處：
    • 節省電費（多數電力公司 PF < 80% 加收費用；> 80% 依規有折扣）。
    • 降低輸入電流，提升線路與變壓器供電能力。
    • 改善供電品質、穩定電壓。
    • 延長電力設備壽命。

English Version

Title

High School Basic Electricity – AC Power – PART D Examples: PT, QT, ST and PF Block Diagram Problems (Two Examples)

Introduction

  • Scope: Use block diagrams to represent load powers, then compute total PT, QT, ST, and PF.
  • Why it matters: Block diagrams help visualize contributions of different loads and avoid sign mistakes in Q.
  • What you’ll learn: Summation strategies for PT/QT, relation to ST, and PF interpretation (leading vs. lagging).

Key Points Summary

  • Block Diagram Problem Type: Represent powers per load to streamline total calculations.
  • Total Active Power (PT): Sum of all loads’ active powers.
  • Total Reactive Power (QT): Absolute difference between inductive and capacitive reactive powers.
  • Total Apparent Power (ST): $ S_T = \sqrt{P_T^2 + Q_T^2} $.
  • Power Factor (PF): $ PF = \frac{P_T}{S_T} $, label as leading or lagging.
  • Example 1: Compute PT, QT, ST, PF directly from given blocks.
  • Example 2: Infer missing values via PF–S relation before summing totals.

中文版

標題

高中基本電學_交流電功率_PART D 範例：PT、QT、ST 及 PF 方塊圖題型（二範例）

前言

  • 主題範圍：用方塊圖呈現各負載功率，進而計算總 PT、QT、ST 與 PF。
  • 為何重要：方塊圖可清楚看出各負載的貢獻，避免 Q 正負號混淆。
  • 你將學到：PT/QT 加總策略、與 ST 之關係，以及 PF（超前/滯後）的判讀。

重點統整

  • 方塊圖題型介紹：以方塊圖表示負載功率，方便計算總功率。
  • 總平均功率 PT 計算：將各負載平均功率相加。
  • 總虛功率 QT 計算：電感虛功率減去電容虛功率的絕對值。
  • 總視在功率 ST 計算：$ S_T = \sqrt{P_T^2 + Q_T^2} $。
  • 功率因數 PF 計算：$ PF = \frac{P_T}{S_T} $，並判斷超前或滯後。
  • 範例一：依方塊圖數據直接計算 PT、QT、ST、PF。
  • 範例二：部分數據缺失，先用 PF 與視在功率關係間接求未知功率，再計算總功率與功率因數。
